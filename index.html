Your privacy matters. This policy explains how LumaOS Pro handles information in connection with its launcher features, personalization options, and permissions. By using the application, you acknowledge this policy.

Depending on the features you use and the permissions you grant, LumaOS Pro may access the following information:

Information accessed by LumaOS Pro is used to provide launcher functionality, display installed applications, apply your personalization choices, remember supported preferences, and operate features you choose to enable.

Personalization preferences and other settings may be stored locally on your device to maintain your customized experience.

LumaOS Pro's handling of information depends on the features and services included in the application.

You can stop using LumaOS Pro at any time by uninstalling the application.

Information stored locally may remain on your device until it is deleted by the application, cleared through device settings, or affected by uninstalling the application.
